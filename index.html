Welcome to My Travel Diaries!

Hi, I'm Jamie! I'm based in Los Angeles and I love traveling, discovering new food, and finding new places to explore. Some of my favorite trips are the ones where I get to experience a place beyond just the usual tourist spots.

I especially love road trips, biking around new cities, trying local food, and taking way too many pictures along the way. Growing up in the Philippines and now living in Los Angeles has also made me interested in experiencing different cultures, food, and ways of life.

Most importantly, I try to bring my French Bulldog, Gucci, whenever the adventure allows it. Whether we're exploring somewhere far from home or just finding a quiet beach in California, I think some of the best memories come from the little adventures in between.
